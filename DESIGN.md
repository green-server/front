# Design

One dark scene drawn as text: the fleet's planet in the middle, rendered in coloured ASCII, each server a satellite on a ring around it, and every screen other than home a framed terminal panel over the scene.

- **Rule:** nothing appears unless it shows real data or leads to an action. No decorative captions, taglines, gradients, glow, radii or shadows.
- **Scene:** `components/OrbitScene.tsx`, one three.js scene mounted above the router that never unmounts. The planet is the real Earth (NASA Blue Marble-based maps from the three.js examples in `public/earth/`, Korea facing the viewer at load). At or below the fleet's 7-day average it looks as it does; above it, `planetHealth` in `lib/orbit.ts` dries the land toward desert, muddies the seas, thins the clouds and turns the air orange. Separately, `planetBlight` blights it with every open recommendation (waste found but not fixed): ashen land, black seas, a grey smog; applying or dismissing one heals it. The ring only exists when there are servers. Phones show the planet smaller, above the content, with no ring. The whole scene is shown as text: it renders offscreen, then a full-screen pass (`ASCII` in `planetShaders.ts`) cuts the frame into 5×9px cells and draws each as the glyph from ` .:-=+*#%@` that matches its brightness, in its own colour, so every effect above still reads. Without WebGL it is the still `public/planet.png`.
- **Satellites:** spaced evenly in server id order; size follows current W, offline servers are small grey dots. No labels: the home view is the planet alone (only a failed load or an empty fleet puts anything over it), and servers are reached through the menu.
- **Panels:** `components/Panel.tsx`, a right-hand panel about 60% wide (full-screen on phones). Its frame is a 1px line through the middle of the edge cells, where box-drawing glyphs draw their strokes, with `← 서버 › 이름` and `[x]` on the top line; only the body scrolls. Opening one moves the planet left. Inside, sections are separated by space and dashed rules.
- **Palette:** tokens at the top of `styles/base.css`. `--paper` background, `--line` frames (3:1), `--ink` / `--ink-2` text, monochrome: `--go` is white, for good and the primary action (`--on-go` on it); the only colours are the planet's and `--warn` / `--bad`, kept for risk and things that need attention. All text meets AA.
- **Type:** one monospace face, D2Coding (OFL, subset woff2 in `fonts/`), for everything; Latin is half a cell (`1ch`), Hangul a whole one, so `ch` and `lh` are the layout units.
- **Controls:** buttons are `[ 승인 ]`, reversed on hover and focus; the primary is filled `--go`. Menus, range pickers and filters reverse the current item. Radios are `( )` / `(•)`; fields sit on a line; carets are `▸` / `▾`; commands are framed with a `$` prompt.
- **실측/추정:** every W value goes through `components/Power.tsx`: a solid underline and 실측 when measured, a leading `~`, dashed underline and 추정 when estimated. The power chart (`components/PowerChart.tsx`) is block characters: measured slices `▁…█` green, estimated `░` grey, peak `▲`. No reading is shown as a measured zero.
- **Motion:** the planet turns, the ring drifts, the camera eases when a panel opens. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` none of it moves. No value depends on an animation.
- **Mark:** `❦` in `--go`, in the top bar and `public/icon.svg`.
