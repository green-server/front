# Design

One dark scene drawn as text: the fleet's planet in the middle, rendered in coloured ASCII, and every screen other than home a framed terminal panel over the scene.

- **Rule:** nothing appears unless it shows real data or leads to an action. No decorative captions, taglines, gradients, glow, radii or shadows.
- **Scene:** `components/OrbitScene.tsx`, one three.js scene mounted above the router that never unmounts. The planet is the real Earth (NASA Blue Marble-based maps from the three.js examples in `public/earth/`, Korea facing the viewer at load). At or below the fleet's 7-day average it looks as it does; above it, `planetHealth` in `lib/orbit.ts` dries the land toward desert, muddies the seas, thins the clouds and turns the air orange. Separately, `planetBlight` blights it with every open recommendation (waste found but not fixed): ashen land, black seas, a grey smog; applying or dismissing one heals it. Phones show the planet smaller, above the content. Home is the planet with one line under it, the fleet's draw now, this month's expected bill and the count of commands waiting for approval, linking to them (a failed load or an empty fleet replaces it); servers are reached through the menu. A small moon circles it on a tilted orbit, lit by the same sun, so it shows phases and passes behind. For about 25 s in every 80 an aurora shimmers around both poles: curtains built from seven stacked shells (about three times the real height, so they stand past the limb), teal at the foot, green through the body, violet at the top, brightest at the limb and on the night side. Among the stars, a few now and then flare into a large four-pointed sparkle (neither under reduced motion). The whole scene is shown as text: it renders offscreen, then a full-screen pass (`ASCII` in `planetShaders.ts`) cuts the frame into 5×9px cells and draws each as the glyph from ` .:-=+*#%@` that matches its brightness, in its own colour, so every effect above still reads. Without WebGL it is the still `public/planet.png`.
- **Panels:** `components/Panel.tsx`, a right-hand panel about 60% wide (full-screen on phones). Its frame is a 1px line through the middle of the edge cells, where box-drawing glyphs draw their strokes, with `← 서버 › 이름` and `[x]` on the top line; only the body scrolls. Opening one moves the planet left. Inside, sections are separated by space and dashed rules.
- **Palette:** tokens at the top of `styles/base.css`. `--paper` background, `--line` frames (3:1), `--ink` / `--ink-2` text, monochrome with one cyan accent: `--go` (`#5fd7ff`) for good and the primary action (`--on-go` on it); `--online` green marks a connected server; the only other colours are the planet's and `--warn` / `--bad`, kept for risk and things that need attention. All text meets AA.
- **Type:** one monospace face, D2Coding (OFL, subset woff2 in `fonts/`), for everything; Latin is half a cell (`1ch`), Hangul a whole one, so `ch` and `lh` are the layout units.
- **Controls:** buttons are `[ 승인 ]`, reversed on hover and focus; the primary is filled `--go`. Menus, range pickers and filters reverse the current item. Radios are `( )` / `(•)`; fields sit on a line; carets are `▸` / `▾`; commands are framed with a `$` prompt.
- **실측/추정:** every W value goes through `components/Power.tsx`: a solid underline and 실측 when measured, a leading `~`, dashed underline and 추정 when estimated. The power chart (`components/PowerChart.tsx`) is block characters: measured slices `▁…█` green, estimated `░` grey, peak `▲`. No reading is shown as a measured zero.
- **Motion:** the planet turns, the camera eases when a panel opens. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` none of it moves. No value depends on an animation.
- **Mark:** `public/icon.svg`, the page icon, shown as-is in the top bar too.
