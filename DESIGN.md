# Design

One dark scene: the fleet's planet in the middle, each server a satellite on a ring around it, and every screen other than home a panel over the scene.

- **Rule:** nothing appears unless it shows real data or leads to an action. No decorative captions, English HUD labels, taglines, metaphors, corner brackets, gradients or glow.
- **Scene:** `components/OrbitScene.tsx`, one three.js scene mounted above the router that never unmounts. The planet is the real Earth (NASA Blue Marble-based maps from the three.js examples in `public/earth/`, Korea facing the viewer at load). At or below the fleet's 7-day average it looks as it does; above it, `planetHealth` in `lib/orbit.ts` dries the land toward desert, muddies the seas, thins the clouds and turns the air orange. The ring only exists when there are servers. Phones show the planet smaller, above the content, with no ring. Without WebGL it is the still `public/planet.png` and the pages list the servers.
- **Satellites:** spaced evenly in server id order; size follows current W, offline servers are small grey dots. The 8 highest-draw servers get labels (`components/SatelliteLabels.tsx`: name, W or 꺼짐) that link to the server; the rest are counted in a "+n" chip that opens `/servers`.
- **Panels:** `components/Panel.tsx`, a right-hand panel about 60% wide (full-screen sheet on phones) with a breadcrumb and a close that goes home. Opening one moves the planet left and dims it. Inside, sections are separated by space and hairlines, not boxes.
- **Palette:** tokens at the top of `styles/base.css`. `--space` background, `--surface` panels, `--line` hairlines, `--ink` / `--ink-2` text, `--go` for good and the primary action (`--on-go` on it), `--warn` and `--bad` only for risk and things that need attention. All text meets AA.
- **Type:** Paperlogy (`--sans`, OFL, local woff2 in `fonts/`) for everything in the UI; IBM Plex Mono (`--code`) only for commands and logs.
- **실측/추정:** every W value goes through `components/Power.tsx`: a solid underline and 실측 when measured, a dashed underline and 추정 when estimated. Charts draw measured runs solid green and estimated runs dashed grey. No reading is shown as 측정값 없음 or —, never as a measured zero.
- **Motion:** the planet turns, the ring drifts, the camera eases when a panel opens, panels slide in, changed digits roll. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` none of it moves: no rotation, no camera easing, no CSS transitions. No value depends on an animation.
- **Logo:** a mark only, a slim leaf with one curved vein (`components/Logo.tsx`), filled with `--logo-mark`; `public/icon.svg` is the favicon.
