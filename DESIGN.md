# Design

A dark forest-green management workspace: persistent navigation, a fleet overview with the existing Earth visualization, and readable server and action lists. Home supports scanning; detail pages use the full content area for work.

- **Rule:** nothing appears unless it shows real data or leads to an action. No decorative captions, English HUD labels, taglines, metaphors, corner brackets, gradients or glow.
- **Scene:** `components/OrbitScene.tsx`, loaded on demand through `components/PlanetView.tsx` on home and authentication screens; detail routes unmount it and release its WebGL context. The planet is the real Earth (NASA Blue Marble-based maps from the three.js examples in `public/earth/`, Korea facing the viewer at load). At or below the fleet's 7-day average it looks as it does; above it, `planetHealth` in `lib/orbit.ts` dries the land toward desert, muddies the seas, thins the clouds and turns the air orange. Separately, `planetBlight` blights it with every open recommendation (waste found but not fixed): ashen land, black seas, a grey smog; applying or dismissing one heals it. The ring only exists when there are servers. Phones show the planet below the readings, with no ring. Without WebGL or the scene chunk, a text fallback appears; the server list remains available.
- **Satellites:** spaced evenly in server id order; size follows current W, offline servers are small grey dots. The 8 highest-draw servers get labels (`components/SatelliteLabels.tsx`: name, W or 꺼짐) that link to the server; the rest are counted in a "+n" chip that opens `/servers`.
- **Panels:** `components/Panel.tsx`, a normal-flow content area with a breadcrumb and a close that goes home. Desktop navigation is a 220px sidebar; phones use a sticky top bar and a bottom navigation bar with safe-area spacing. Inside, sections are separated by space and hairlines, not boxes.
- **Palette:** tokens at the top of `styles/base.css`. `--space: #101413` background, `--surface: #181e1b` surfaces, `--line` hairlines, `--ink: #eff3ed` / `--ink-2: #a0ada5` text, `--go: #b6ed80` for good and the primary action (`--on-go: #04140b` on it), `--warn` and `--bad` only for risk and things that need attention. All text meets AA.
- **Type:** Paperlogy (`--sans`, OFL, local woff2 in `fonts/`) for everything in the UI; IBM Plex Mono (`--code`) only for commands and logs.
- **실측/추정:** every W value goes through `components/Power.tsx`: a solid underline and 실측 when measured, a dashed underline and 추정 when estimated. Charts draw measured runs solid green and estimated runs dashed grey. No reading is shown as 측정값 없음 or —, never as a measured zero.
- **Motion:** the planet turns, the ring drifts, and changed digits roll. Page content has no entrance animation. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` none of it moves: no rotation, no camera easing, no CSS transitions. No value depends on an animation.
- **Logo:** a mark only, a slim leaf with one curved vein (`components/Logo.tsx`), filled with `--logo-mark`; `public/icon.svg` is the favicon.

- **Overview:** live power, monthly forecast and connected counts above a shared `ServerList` and pending approval/recommendation links. Loading, errors with retry, and empty states are explicit. The Earth never blocks navigation or data.
- **Authentication:** a split visual and form on desktop, compact introduction and stacked fields on phones. Labels remain visible; fields and the submit action have 48px targets.
